Frequently asked questions

How do I remove ads?

Settings → the gold Remove ads button. It's a one-time purchase that disables all ads permanently.

I bought "Remove ads" but ads are back (new device / reinstall)

Open Settings → Restore purchases. The purchase is tied to your Apple ID, so it restores for free on any of your devices.

How does multiplayer work?

  • One Device — two players on one screen, each guards their side.
  • Nearby — two iPhones next to each other, over local Wi-Fi/Bluetooth. If no one is found, allow Local Network access for ArcanoBattle in iOS Settings on both devices.
  • Online — via Game Center anywhere in the world. Both players need to be signed in to Game Center (iOS Settings → Game Center) and search at the same time.

Online says "Could not establish a connection"

Some networks block the traffic Game Center matches need. Try switching between Wi-Fi and cellular (on either side) and search again.

The ball or the game froze / crashed

Please tell us what happened at kry.sup@proton.me — the device model, what you were doing, and a screenshot if possible. It helps a lot.
