ArcanoBattle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

ArcanoBattle ("the Game") is developed by indie kry (Individual Entrepreneur Raman Krauchuk, Georgia). This policy explains what data is processed when you play the Game and what choices you have.

Data we do NOT collect

The Game has no accounts and no registration. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment details. We do not use the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Advertising

The Game is free thanks to ads. Ads are served by third-party SDKs, which may process the following technical data on their servers: a device identifier (identifier for vendor), IP address, approximate region derived from it, device model and OS version, and ad interaction events (impressions, clicks).

  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) — privacy policy
  • Yandex Mobile Ads / AppMetrica (Yandex) — privacy policy

On first launch the Game asks whether you allow personalized ads. If you decline, ads remain non-personalized. You can also remove all ads permanently with a one-time in-app purchase.

Analytics and diagnostics

Anonymous usage statistics and crash/performance diagnostics may be collected by the SDKs above to keep the Game stable. This data is not linked to your identity.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment information. The "Remove Ads" purchase is stored on your device and in your Apple account (it can be restored on a new device via "Restore Purchases").

Game Center

Online multiplayer uses Apple Game Center. Your Game Center nickname is visible to the player you are matched with. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Depending on your region (e.g., GDPR in the EU), you may have the right to access or delete data processed by the ad SDK providers listed above; please refer to their privacy policies for the corresponding requests. You can change nothing-to-personalized ad consent by reinstalling the Game (the consent screen is shown again on first launch).

Changes

We may update this policy as the Game evolves; the current version is always available at this address.
